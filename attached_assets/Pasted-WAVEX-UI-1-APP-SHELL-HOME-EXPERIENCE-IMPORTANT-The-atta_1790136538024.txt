CORE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
==================================================

SOPHISTICATED UNDER THE HOOD.

==================================================
SCREEN D — HOME ERROR
==================================================

Follow the third phone in the reference.

If the portfolio genuinely fails:

Show:

Couldn't load your balance.

Secondary text:

Check your connection and try again.

Primary action:

Retry

Use the existing refresh/read mechanism.

Do not expose technical errors such as:

NETWORK_UNAVAILABLE
eth_chainId
RPC
JSON-RPC
FetchRpcTransport

on the primary Home surface.

Development-only Preview diagnostics may remain available through existing development mechanisms.

IMPORTANT:

Do not convert every error into zero.

Only successful zero balance becomes:

AVAILABLE ZERO.

==================================================
ZERO BALANCE STATE
==================================================

This is a strict requirement.

If:

eth_getBalance succeeds

and returns:

0x0

the portfolio is AVAILABLE.

Home must show:

0 balance

and appropriate zero fiat value:

$0.00

or equivalent.

It must NOT show:

Blockchain data unavailable.

The following distinction must remain intact:

AVAILABLE + ZERO
≠
UNAVAILABLE
